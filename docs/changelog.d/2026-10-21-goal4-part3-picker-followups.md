## 2026-10-21 - Four small fixes found while building the record pickers (Goal 4, part 3 follow-ups)

Frontend only, no migration. Each was flagged during PR #1017's reviews as pre-existing behaviour.

- **Quote Builder.** When a new customer clears a section's field because it belongs to someone else, the quote lines drop the acres that field filled in: a rate line's total goes to zero (rate times no acres) and a units line keeps its total but loses its per-acre figures. Nothing is re-priced, so this does not wait for products or unit conversions. Acres typed by hand are kept, even when they equal the field's. A line loaded from a saved quote has no record of where its acres came from, so its acres are kept (guessing could overwrite typed acres and bill them) and the user is warned to check those lines' acres; recording the source needs a database column (follow-up). A saved field cannot be cleared or replaced while it is still loading, and one whose read failed stays locked until the page is refreshed. This applies when the customer is picked, when a saved field finishes loading after a customer was picked, and when the section's field is cleared or replaced by hand (a replacement then fills in its own acres). Just opening a quote, or switching to another one, never clears its field.
- **AR Aging statement.** A customer with no transactions in the chosen range now shows "No transactions in this date range." after Load Transactions, instead of the "select a customer" prompt. A loaded statement shows only for the customer and dates it was loaded for: changing either, or going Back to Aging, returns to the prompt until it is loaded again (before, the old rows stayed up under the new period line). The statement opened from an aging row now reports a failed load to Sentry.
- **Quick Delivery.** Its customer search quotes the typed text (`ilikeOrPattern`), so a farm name with a comma no longer breaks the search; a failed search is reported to Sentry, and a reply for older search text is dropped.
- **Job Detail.** Loading a recipe or crop program, or picking a product on a chemical line, prices at the job customer's own tier. The customer list holds active customers only, so a job for an inactive customer reads that customer's tier by id. If the tier cannot be read, is still loading, or no customer is chosen yet, pricing is blocked with a message instead of falling back to tier 1. A product with no price for a tier-2 or tier-3 customer is priced at its tier-1 price, never at $0, for recipes, crop programs and product picks; a price stated as $0 stays $0 (`getStatedTierPrice` in `src/lib/quoteCalc.ts`). A picked product's price, $0 included, replaces the previous product's.
