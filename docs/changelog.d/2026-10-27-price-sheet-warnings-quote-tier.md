## 2026-10-27 — The sent-quote warning only counts a quote whose own tier price moves (To-Do 0.1, Codex App on PR #1131)

- Migration `20261027050000_price_sheet_warnings_and_approval.sql` (not applied): `OPEN_SENT_QUOTE` used to flag every open unlocked quote holding the product whenever any tier's price changed. `save_quote` prices a line at the quote's own tier (`CASE v_tier`: 1, 2, anything else tier 3; a blank tier 2/3 price falls back to tier 1), so a tier 2 change cannot reach a tier 1 quote. The warning now joins each quote to its own tier's before/after price and counts it only when that price moves.
- The apply-time re-read (`_pricing_change_set_warning_inputs`) now lists each open quote with its tier (`open_quotes: [[id, tier], ...]`, was `open_quote_ids`). It cannot re-derive which tiers moved (the engine has written the new prices by its second read), so a quote added, removed or moved to another tier during apply refuses (preview again); the exact per-tier rule is re-checked by the recomputed warning set before apply.
- Proof: a new scenario (a tier-2-only move does not flag the tier 1 quote; after the quote moves to tier 2 the same move does, and a tier 1 move does not), and the stated-$0 tier 2 step no longer expects the tier 1 quote to be flagged.
