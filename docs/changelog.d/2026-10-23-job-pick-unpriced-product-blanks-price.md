## 2026-10-23 - Job Detail: picking a product with no price no longer keeps the old price

Job Detail screen plus one migration (`20261022087000_job_chemicals_refuse_unpriced_zero_price_line.sql`, history row 981). Follow-up from PR #1049.

- **Job Detail.** Picking a product on a chemical line that states no price (no price for the job customer's tier and no tier-1 price) used to leave the previous product's price on the line, so the new product could be billed at the old product's price. The line's price is now blanked, and the existing "This line cannot be saved" check refuses the save until a price is typed. A product priced at $0 still sets the line to exactly $0. Loading a recipe or crop program works the same way: a line whose product states no price (and, for a recipe, has no recipe price) arrives with a blank price instead of $0, so a recipe saved from such a line cannot slip a $0 price past the check. A customer-supplied line is never billed, so its blank price does not block the save (it is saved as $0). Un-ticking "customer supplied" on a line at $0 (or blank) re-prices it from the catalog at the customer's tier (a stated $0 included) when the line is billed in the product's own stock unit (`priceTextInLineUnit`), and blanks it for any other unit, because converting would round the price; a recipe line that falls back to the catalog price follows the same rule; if the product states no price, or the tier is not known, the price is blanked, so the line cannot go back to billing at $0. On a product pick, a recipe or crop program load, or un-ticking, a positive catalog price that would round to $0 when converted to a smaller unit (for example $0.50 a gallon priced per ounce) is also left blank for a price to be typed, not filled in as $0 (`autofillPriceText` in `src/lib/chemCalculator.ts`). The database enforces it too (migration `20261022087000`, Mason's choice): a trigger on `job_chemicals` refuses a billable line priced $0 whose product states no price for the job customer's tier (`CHEM_PRICE_REQUIRED`, shown as "Nothing was saved. …"), whichever path writes it (Job Detail's save, the old recipe RPC, scheduling a job from a quote, or a direct write), so an older app or a direct call cannot store it either.
