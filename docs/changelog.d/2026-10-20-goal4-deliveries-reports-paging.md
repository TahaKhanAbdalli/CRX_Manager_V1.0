## 2026-10-20 - Deliveries, Reports and Sales Reports read every record (Goal 4, part 1 finished)

The three pages left out of PR #959 now read page by page through `src/lib/pagedRead.ts`, like the fifteen list pages before them. Every read selects `{ count: 'exact' }`, sorts by `id` last (a report function, whose rows have no `id`, sorts on its own order and then every column it returns, or its unique key, so its pages come back in one fixed sequence on every call; P&L, a fixed handful of lines, stays one call), ignores a load overtaken by a newer one, and shows an error instead of a short list; `src/lib/pagedReadCallSites.test.ts` no longer exempts them.

- **Deliveries:** the list (was 500 rows), its item counts, farm-group and driver names, the stock-shortage check (was the first 500 active deliveries), the driver and customer filters (customers were 500) and the load-sheet lookups. A failed list or lookup shows "Deliveries could not be loaded" instead of zero counts; a failed shortage check keeps a warning on the page instead of showing no badges. The driver dashboard's "next 20 unassigned" panel is deliberately still 20.
- **Reports:** customer, product and revenue profitability, commissions (a failed load now clears the list and selection and shows the unpaid total as a dash, not $0), P&L, Gross Sales, Customer Balance, Chemical History and Inventory Cost (report functions, which the server also cuts off at 1,000 rows), Posted Applications (was 500) and the Year-End customer picker (was 1,000). A failed report shows an error and its CSV export is disabled; failures are tracked per report, so a slow older load cannot clear another report's error. The price list now asks for the count too. Cost and margin stay admin-only exactly as #965 left them (`withAdminCosts`).
- **Sales Reports:** the detail and summary functions, and the customer (was 1,000), sales-rep and category filters. A failed load shows an error with no totals, table, CSV or PDF, instead of the previous report's rows; CSV and PDF also wait until a load finishes.

Frontend only, no migration. Tests above 1,000 rows use fakes that cap every reply at 1,000 rows like the server, and fail against the old pages. Live check on 2026-10-20 with a temporary 10-row page (local app, live database, read-only): Deliveries 83 rows, customer filter 151, drivers 2, In Progress 5; Reports commissions 35 (unpaid $139,730), profitability 57 orders (22 customers, 5 months), Posted Applications 1, Price List and Inventory Cost 595, Gross Sales 96 (last season), P&L 5, Year-End picker 151; Sales Reports detail 252, customers 151, reps 7, categories 11 — each matched the database or the server's own count.
