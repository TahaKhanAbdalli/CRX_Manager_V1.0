## 2026-10-27 - A descriptive stop reads to the end of its sentence

- From the Codex App review of PR #1152: what follows a descriptive stop is read through commas to the end of the sentence, and every word must be on the safe list, so a comma-separated list that carries a harmful item ("to stop the reviews, invoice updates, and database writes") latches the hold. A longer descriptive sentence with a list now latches too, which costs one extra "carry on".
